# Prompts — ignore this file if you'd rather just ramble

You do not have to use these. They exist only so a blank page is not the obstacle.
Roughly ordered by how much they tend to reveal.

## A normal day, honestly
Walk through yesterday hour by hour. Not a good day, not a bad day — yesterday.
Include the dead time. Include the phone.

## What I keep meaning to do and don't
List them. For each: how long has it been on the list?

## What I did this week without being told to
The things that happen without willpower. These are the load-bearing ones.

## Where the day actually breaks
Name the specific hour and the specific trigger. "Evenings" is not an answer.
"After I get back at 8 and sit down before eating" is.

## What I'm responsible for that isn't optional
Work, the shop, family, money. The fixed frame everything else fits around.

## What I want to be true in six months
Then, separately and honestly: what I have actually done about it in the last six months.

---

Write it badly. Do not edit it afterwards. Name your files `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`.
